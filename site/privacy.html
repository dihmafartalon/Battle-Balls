Battle Balls privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

What the game keeps

Your progress (coins, yen, rank points, the blades, abilities and skins you own, your stats and the name you pick in the game) is saved in your own browser on the device you play on.

If you choose to sign in with Google, a copy of that progress is also kept on the game's server so it follows you to other devices. It is stored under the ID number Google gives your account for this game. The server never stores your email address, your real name or your picture.

What we use from Google

Only the basic sign-in, to know it is the same account each time. The game does not read your email, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account, and never posts anything for you.

Multiplayer

Playing online needs you to be signed in with Google. The name you pick and your loadout are shown to the other players in your room while the room is open. So that cheaters can be found and banned, the server notes which rooms each account played in (the last few, with the other players' in-game names), and keeps a short record whenever its anti-cheat notices something, saying what and when. It never records chat, your voice, or anything outside the game.

Fair play

Coins, yen, rank points and items are handled by the game's server, which also checks match rewards, abilities and blocking for signs of cheating. An account caught cheating can be banned from saving and playing online, and is told why. If you think a ban is a mistake, contact the developer (below).

What we never do

  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • We never sell or share your information with anyone.
  • No analytics or tracking cookies.

Where it is stored

The site and the save server run on Cloudflare. Google handles the sign-in itself.

Removing your data

In the game, Profile → Sign out stops saving to your account, and Reset progress clears this device. To have your cloud save deleted as well, contact the developer at the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Battle Balls and it will be removed.
